GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Here are a few things clients often want to know.

How much does a website cost? +

Pricing depends on page count, design, integrations and features. Share your requirements and we'll discuss a suitable scope and estimate.

How long does a project take? +

A simple website usually takes less time than a custom web app or e-commerce project. We'll agree on milestones after understanding your needs.

Can you update my existing website? +

Yes. We can discuss a redesign, responsive improvements, new pages, performance improvements or integrations.

Will I be able to manage the website? +

We can plan an appropriate editing approach and provide a handover for the parts of the site included in the project.
